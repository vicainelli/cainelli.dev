Slotta is a side project I am building to make booking painless for small service businesses and their clients.

It started from a frustrating personal experience: booking a haircut required installing a native app first. For a 20-second task, that feels like unnecessary friction.

The goal is simple: give barbers, hairdressers, massage therapists, and other independent professionals a booking page they can share as a link. Customers pick a slot, confirm, and done — no download, no account, no hassle.

I am documenting the build publicly in the [Building in Public](/me/building-in-public) series.
