I joined a small digital agency as its lead web developer, building websites, internal systems, and marketing platforms for clients across different industries. Working directly with designers, marketers, and clients taught me how technical decisions affect business outcomes and the overall user experience.

I designed and developed websites, landing pages, internal systems, custom CMSs, and CRM-style applications using PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. When existing platforms were more complex than needed, I built lightweight content management systems tailored to each client's workflow.

I also worked closely with marketing teams to improve SEO, implement Google Tag Manager, and optimise website performance. Most projects went from initial requirements through development and deployment, often with me as the sole developer. That taught me to be resourceful and to own outcomes end to end.
