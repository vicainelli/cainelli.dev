I was looking for an app that records meetings locally, transcribes them, and uses AI to summarize them. Today I found Anarlog, and it's easy and straightforward to set up. I'm really happy with it.

My setup: Deepgram models handle the transcription, and Claude Haiku 5.5 (through my Copilot subscription) turns the transcript into a summary.

[Try Anarlog](https://anarlog.so/?ref=cainelli.dev)
