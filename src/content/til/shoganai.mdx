For the past few years, I’ve tried to live by Stoicism: understand what’s within my control, act on what I can change, and accept what I can’t.

A few days ago, I came across the Japanese concept of _shōganai_ — roughly, “it can’t be helped.”

Something happened. It’s done. If there’s nothing you can do about it, there’s little value in continuing to fight it in your head.

It feels like a natural companion to Stoicism.

Stoicism asks: **What can I control?**

_Shōganai_ reminds me: **And if I can’t, let it go.**

> Change what you can. Accept what you can’t. Move on.
